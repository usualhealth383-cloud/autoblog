# 약지도 07 · 증상 안내 본문 번역 영어 2편 · 출시 전
증상 안내 본문(먹는 법·응급 신호 포함)의 번역 검수 — 응급·금기 조건이 약해지지 않았는지.

```
약사·가정의학과 전문의이자 영어(English) 원어민 의료 번역가로서, 한국 약 안내 앱(약지도)의 증상 안내 본문 번역을 검수해 주세요(2/4). 응급·금기 문장은 이미 검수를 마쳤고, 이번은 나머지(요약·약 고르는 법·먹는 법·약국에서·집에서·자주 묻는 질문)입니다.

답 형식: 문제 있는 줄만 「id · 무엇이 문제 · 고친 영어(English) 문장」(긴 글은 고칠 문장만). 문제 없으면 「이상 없음」.

EN: **Tension-type headache and migraine often come mixed together rather than separately.** When neck and shoulder muscles are knotted hard as a rock, the pain climbs from the back of the neck into the head. Doctors sometimes give a muscle relaxant for this, but **European headache guidelines don't recommend muscle relaxants for tension-type headache.** Try a 10-minute warm compress and neck and shoulder stretches first.

Pharmacies carry combination products with chlorzoxazone, and doctors use eperisone. If it makes you drowsy or keeps you awake, talk with your pharmacist about when to take it (some products contain caffeine).

Pharmacy muscle-relaxant combinations contain a pain-relief ingredient called **ethenzamide**, so the package insert says not to take them with other fever reducers and pain relievers or cold medicines. This product's package insert says not to take it at the same time as other fever reducers and pain relievers or cold medicines. Doctors sometimes use them together, but check with your pharmacist or doctor first before taking them together.

Rinse your nose with saline and try a decongestant nasal spray for no more than three days. See the [[symptom:rhinitis|stuffy nose page]] too. If thick yellow mucus lasts more than ten days or the fever comes back, it's sinusitis, so see a doctor.
```
